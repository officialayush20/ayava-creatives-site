## 2. How We Price (Core Philosophy)

**Left statement (H2, cols 1–4):**
A fixed package is a guess about your account before we've looked at it.

**Right paragraphs (cols 5–12):**

Fixed pricing tiers work when every customer needs roughly the same thing. Marketing doesn't work that way. A single-location business running one paid channel and a five-market e-commerce brand running six don't belong on the same price sheet — put them there anyway and one of them overpays for capacity they don't use, while the other gets a scope too thin to move the number that matters.

So we price by scope, not by tier. Before we name a figure, we look at which services the work actually needs, how much campaign volume has to be supported, and whether the engagement is a defined project or an ongoing channel to manage. The quote comes out of that scoping conversation — it isn't selected off a menu.

This takes slightly longer than handing over a rate card. It also means the number you get is the number your account actually needs, not the number that happened to be printed on a tier closest to your budget.

## 3. What Shapes a Quote

**Eyebrow:** What Goes Into a Quote
**H2:** Three things determine the number.

- **Services selected** — Which of Ayava's 15 services — or which combination — the engagement actually needs. A Meta Ads account and a full website rebuild are scoped, and priced, differently.
- **Campaign scale** — Ad spend, channel count, and content volume the work has to support. A single-channel launch and a multi-market always-on program carry different operational loads.
- **Engagement shape** — Whether the work is a one-time project with a defined end or an ongoing retainer that runs month over month. See Engagement Models below.

---

## 4. Engagement Models

**Eyebrow:** How Engagements Are Structured
**H2:** Three ways to work with us.

**Project-Based**
A defined scope with a start and an end. You know what's being delivered and when it's done. Fits a website build, a brand identity, or a single campaign launch — work with a clear finish line, not an open-ended commitment.

**Ongoing Retainer**
Continuous management of a channel or set of channels. Fits always-on paid media, ongoing SEO, or content programs that need consistent attention month over month rather than a one-time delivery.

**One-Time Audit**
A scoped diagnostic engagement with a defined deliverable and no ongoing commitment. Fits an account or creative audit — useful when you want an outside, expert read on what's working before deciding what (if anything) to change.
